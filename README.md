# Privacy Policy for Hearth (温度)

**Last updated:** May 16, 2026

Hearth ("we", "our", "the app") is a recipe and meal planning app that lets individuals and families create, store, and share recipes. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using Hearth, you agree to the practices described below.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We only collect what's needed for the app to work. Specifically:

### Account Information
When you create an account or sign in with Apple, we collect:
- **Email address** — used to identify your account and send you account-related emails (e.g. password resets).
- **Display name** — shown to other members of any family groups you join.
- **A unique user ID** — generated automatically when your account is created.

If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, Apple provides us with a relay address instead of your real email. We treat this the same as any other email.

### Content You Create
When you use Hearth, we store the content you create:
- **Recipes** — title, description, ingredients, steps, category, tags, calories, photos
- **Family groups** — group names, photos, member lists, join codes
- **Meal plans** — recipes you add to your weekly planner
- **Profile photo** — if you upload one

### Photos
If you grant permission, the app accesses your **camera** and **photo library** to add photos to recipes, group profiles, or your own profile. Photos you choose to upload are stored on our servers and shared with members of any family groups you've shared the recipe with. Photos remain on your device until you upload them.

### Device Information
The app stores small amounts of data on your device using standard iOS storage:
- Your selected color theme and language preference
- A local copy of your recipes so the app works offline

We do **not** collect device identifiers, location, contacts, browsing history, or any other personal data.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only to:
- Operate the app's core functionality (storing and syncing your recipes, groups, and meal plans)
- Authenticate your account
- Show your name, avatar, and contributions to other members of family groups you've joined
- Send account-related emails (password reset, etc.)

We do **not**:
- Sell your data
- Share your data with advertisers
- Use your data for marketing or profiling
- Track you across other apps or websites

---

## 3. How Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored using [Supabase](https://supabase.com), a cloud platform that hosts our database, file storage, and authentication. Supabase stores data in secure data centers and encrypts data both in transit and at rest.

Photos and group/profile images are stored in Supabase Storage and are accessible to members of any family group you choose to share them with.

If you delete a recipe, photo, or your account, the corresponding data is removed from our servers.

---

## 4. Sharing With Family Groups

Hearth is built around sharing recipes with people you choose. When you share a recipe with a family group:
- Other members of that group can view the recipe, including its photos
- Your display name and avatar appear as the author
- Members can add the recipe to their own meal planner

Recipes you mark as **Private** are only visible to you. You control which recipes are private and which are shared, and you can change this at any time.

---

## 6. Your Choices and Rights

You can:
- **View and edit** your recipes, profile, and group memberships at any time inside the app
- **Delete** any recipe, photo, or group you created
- **Leave** any family group you've joined
- **Export** all your recipes as a JSON or Markdown backup from Settings → Backup & Restore
- **Delete your account** — contact us using the email below to permanently delete your account and all associated data

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as GDPR (EU) or CCPA (California), including the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to request a copy. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

Hearth is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.
